An extra receptacle does not establish extra capacity

Receptacle locations and circuit design answer different questions. More convenient plug-in points may share the same existing circuit. New appliances or specialty equipment may need a different connection, a dedicated circuit or other assessed work. Give the Licensed Electrical Contractor the equipment information and intended location so the proposal follows the actual use.

Prepare the scope for your Grimsby property

Photograph each receptacle in the bathroom or damp area from a short distance, noting any test or reset buttons, discolouration or loose faceplates. Write down what is plugged in there and whether the spot is splashed or only near water.

Ask the contractor whether the receptacles in that location should be ground-fault protected, how the protection is provided, and what written note will show the device type and where it was installed.

What should the assessment establish?

Each location in a Grimsby bathroom or damp spot is listed with the work proposed for it. If a device and its cover suit the exposure, the record shows it as found; if not, that is noted beside that location. The protection each receptacle has now is identified, together with the circuit that serves the location and whatever else is on it. A damaged or discoloured device is reported as a finding rather than guessed about. The list is dated, so a lender or an insurer who reads it later sees what was found and what was changed.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Should a worn bathroom outlet be dealt with before a listing?

Stop using a device that shows heat, discolouration or a smell and leave it alone. The contractor can assess it and record the finding, which is more useful than a cosmetic fix before a buyer's inspector looks.

What should a record of damp-location receptacles show?

It should show each location, the device type, what protection it has and the date and contractor of any work. Photographs of each device support the written entry.
