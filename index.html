What should I send when an insurer asks about my wiring?

Ask the insurer what it needs in writing first. Then offer records you hold, such as ESA paperwork, invoices and exterior photographs of the panel door and labels, plus a written contractor assessment if the insurer asks for one.

Does a lender need a different record than an insurer?

It may. Each reader has its own purpose, so confirm each request separately and keep the wording of its questions beside your reply. One contractor report can answer both only if its stated purpose and scope cover both.

Which quotes are comparable when a lender wants an answer soon?

Give each contractor the same written request, then compare what each scope covers, what it leaves out and what paperwork it will provide. A quote that omits the written result may answer neither the lender nor the insurer.

Can one assessment cover the panel and an EV charger plan?

Only if the written purpose names both. Ask the contractor to separate findings by equipment, so a charger plan does not blur the answer about the existing panel that a lender or an insurer asked about.

Does sending the form settle anything with a contractor?

No: the form sends an enquiry, reviewed on arrival. The website offers guides to read, with no hand in visits, pricing or electrical work. Should you later contact a provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor, it confirms its business name and licence separately; you can verify the licence through ESA.
